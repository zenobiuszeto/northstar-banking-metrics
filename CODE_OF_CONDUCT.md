# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project community. Be considerate, discuss ideas and evidence rather than people, respect privacy, and avoid sharing confidential or regulated information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted sexual attention, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct is unsafe or disruptive.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. The owner must publish a named enforcement contact before opening the project to external contributors. Reports should be handled confidentially, promptly, consistently, and without retaliation.
